[Hobbies]({{ '/hobbies/' | relative_url }}) / Mountaineering

I first started climbing mountains for a simple reason: to find a place with a sufficiently dark sky and a wide, unobstructed field of view for stargazing.

These photographs capture alpine ridges and rock routes, often shared with friends.
