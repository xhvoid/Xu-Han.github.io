My interests in the night sky and the mountains have always been connected.

## Astronomical Photography

Looking at the stars was the *initial perturbation* that set me on the path towards physics, mountaineering and photonics. This gallery brings together images from Wendelstein Observatory, my DSLR astrophotography and nights under alpine skies.

## Mountaineering

I first started climbing mountains for a simple reason: to find a place with a sufficiently dark sky and a wide, unobstructed field of view for stargazing. These photographs capture alpine ridges and rock routes, often shared with friends.
