\section{Selected Research and Engineering Projects}
\begin{onecolentry}
{\large\textbf{Shack-Hartmann Wavefront Sensing \& Adaptive Optics Simulation}}
\end{onecolentry}
\begin{twocolentry}{\textit{Mar 2026 - Present}}
\textit{Independent project} | \hrefWithoutArrow{https://xhvoid.github.io/Shack-Hartmann-AO-Simulation/}{Interactive explorer} | \hrefWithoutArrow{https://github.com/xhvoid/Shack-Hartmann-AO-Simulation}{Source code}
\end{twocolentry}
\begin{onecolentry}
\begin{highlights}
\item Developed a modular Python SCAO framework connecting atmospheric turbulence, detector-level Shack-Hartmann sensing, calibrated reconstruction, deformable-mirror correction and delayed feedback.
\item Traced four faint spots in a simulated 42 m telescope image to waffle modes the sensor barely detects. Filtering those modes reduced their mean brightness from 10.8 to 0.91 times the local halo level.
\item Diagnosed a reconstruction failure associated with measurement dropout in a 2.2 m NGS model; stronger TSVD regularisation kept reconstruction usable throughout the matched two-second run.
\item Cross-checked selected optical components against HCIPy and analytic references; documented reproducible experiments, model limitations and an interactive explorer of recorded results.
\end{highlights}
\end{onecolentry}
\vspace{0.08 cm}
\begin{twocolentry}{\textit{2026}}
{\large\textbf{\href{https://xhvoid.github.io/Xu-Han.github.io/projects/elt-owl/}{Optical Reconstruction of ESO’s 100 m OWL Telescope}}}
\end{twocolentry}
\begin{onecolentry}
\textit{Independent extension of a Zemax laboratory project}
\end{onecolentry}
\begin{onecolentry}
\begin{highlights}
\item Rebuilt the proposed OverWhelmingly Large Telescope from ESO’s Blue Book prescription. At 5 arcmin off axis, the RMS wavefront error was 1.472 µm, within 0.3\% of ESO’s 1.476 µm.
\item At 3 arcmin, traced a 27-percentage-point discrepancy in ensquared energy to the focal surface. A spherical surface brought the model within 0.4 percentage points of ESO’s sampled curve.
\end{highlights}
\end{onecolentry}
